CF Project

Show up.
Earn the shirt.

A fitness challenge created to keep you consistent, motivated and showing up. Complete your sessions and earn exclusive CF and Capra Gang T-shirts as your reward.

  • Project 20: 1 gym session = 1 point. 20 points = a free T-shirt from CF Gear.
  • You can also earn points by winning games.
  • Almost 500 shirts given away since season 1.
  • No more paper card. Scan the QR code at the gym and your point is added. Your card lives on your phone, so it can't get lost.
